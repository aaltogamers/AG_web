Quick info for those who missed the Welcome Evening

Want to hold a gaming event for your favorite game? Want to have a good time with people who share similar interests?

You should consider becoming an Aalto Gamers Active!

AG Actives (& the Board) are the people who plan and organize all AG events! 
More info on AG and being an Active in the below link:
https://aaltogamers.fi/link/info

Perks of being an AG Active:
Learn about event organizing, streaming, partnerships & more
24/7 access to the AG Club Room
Monthly activity days, where the actives & the board do some fun activity, and get to know each other better
No commitment, can help with events just a bit, or a lot
Have fun, gain lifelong friends!

If you're interested, join up from the link below:
https://aaltogamers.fi/link/active
